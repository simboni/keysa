Six ways in

Choose your way to show up

Become a mentor

Guide a young person through education, enterprise or early career. Our mentorship programs pair professionals with youth in TVET, agriculture, business, sports and the arts — a few hours a month changes trajectories.

Remote-friendly2–4 hrs/month

Coach or teach a skill

Football, volleyball, athletics, music, drama, art, tech, financial literacy — if you can teach it, our youth want to learn it. Join a training weekend or run a recurring workshop.

On the groundSkills-based

Join climate action days

Tree-planting drives, community nurseries, climate-smart farm demonstrations and clean-energy builds. Perfect for groups, schools and corporate volunteering days.

Groups welcomeHands-on

Partner as an organisation

TVET institutions, businesses, foundations, county government, faith communities — we co-design programs with partners and report like we mean it. Sponsorship packages available for tournaments and showcases.

InstitutionalCo-designed

Spread the word

Follow and share our work. Introduce us to one person who should know about KEYSA — a donor, an employer who hires our graduates, a journalist. Word of mouth is our oldest program.

5 minutesAnywhere

Give — once or monthly

The fastest way to help. Fund a scholarship, kit a team, or make a monthly pledge that lets us plan ahead with confidence.

What you can expect

We take volunteers as seriously as donors

  • A named board contact and a clear role description before you start
  • Safeguarding-first culture — we work with vulnerable young people and act like it
  • Real responsibility: our programs are volunteer-powered, not volunteer-decorated
  • Recognition and references for sustained contribution

Ready when you are

One email starts the conversation. Tell us what you’re good at and how much time you have — we’ll suggest the best fit.
